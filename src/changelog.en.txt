# GameRemix change log. Important things only: new games, new features, milestones. Small fixes and corrections are not listed (AGENTS.md, item 13).
## 1.0.33 — 2026-11-07
- Quick setup: items right after upscaling, a new "Effect shaders" step (experimental, off by default) with the F9, F10, F11 keys

## 1.0.32 — 2026-11-07
- Fixes are installed afresh from the original game files: updating works correctly even after skipping several versions of the program

## 1.0.31 — 2026-11-07
- The Play button reminds you of new versions of the installed sets and offers to update them before playing

## 1.0.30 — 2026-11-07
- FPS mode: fire, torches, candles and fireplaces burn calmer, as intended, the world runs a steady 40 steps a second
- Quick setup turns the FPS mode on by default

## 1.0.29 — 2026-11-07
- When a new version of an installed set (items, interface, etc.) comes out, the program offers to update it in one click

## 1.0.28 — 2026-11-07
- FPS mode (80 Hz tick) refined

## 1.0.27 — 2026-11-07
- FPS mode: townsfolk walking about now move smoothly at 80 Hz too

## 1.0.26 — 2026-11-07
- Backpack sorting on Ctrl+C (or with the Sort button)

## 1.0.25 — 2026-11-07
- Quick setup now offers the redrawn items (they are the default) and, on 2K and 4K monitors, switches to larger fonts by itself

## 1.0.20 — 2026-11-07
- "FPS mod" (80 Hz tick): a new tab in Effects and FPS and a step in Quick setup together with item glow

## 1.0.19 — 2026-11-07
- "Large windows" now work with every interface - Parchment, Dark metal, Dark glass, Original HD, Redrawn - and together with Clean graphics (v2)

## 1.0.18 — 2026-11-07
- Quick setup is now a step-by-step wizard: monitor, interface, upscaling, fonts, large windows, items - every step shows pictures of the result, and one Apply at the end shows the progress
- "Large windows" (Off | On) on the Graphics tab - one kit: a big backpack with the mouse wheel, trading with tiles and tabs, a Sort button (Ctrl+C) and a stats window that fits the large fonts; the equipment and backpack windows no longer open on top of each other
- Redrawn items: 634 pictures of weapons, armour and things in the new "Redrawn" set (and "Original HD")
- "Item glow" (Off | On, on by default): a coloured halo under green, yellow and gold items in the backpack and coloured Alt labels
- The character stats window is bigger and fits the large fonts

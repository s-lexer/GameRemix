Interface size (picture scale)	인터페이스 크기(화면 배율)
The game is drawn at a SMALLER resolution and the whole frame is then stretched to your monitor, so everything gets bigger at once: the text windows and the world.\nExample: a 2560x1440 monitor at 125% - the game is drawn at 2048x1152 and stretched to the full screen.\nThe higher the percent, the bigger the text, but the less of the world you see around the hero.\nA whole factor (200%: one game pixel = exactly 2x2 screen pixels) gives the sharpest picture. A fractional one (125%, 150%) does not divide evenly, so a smoothing filter is needed and the picture is slightly softer.\n"Auto" picks the scale from the monitor height: under 1300 px - 100%, from 1300 - 125%, from 2000 - 200%.\nChange it if the text is too small (raise it) or the world feels cramped (lower it).
